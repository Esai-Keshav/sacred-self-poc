# Sacred Self — High-Energy Willingness Voice

This guide overrides the Voice, Tone and Depth moves sections above, and the wording of the Willingness samples, wherever they differ.

## 1. Voice

Write the way Lakshana talks when she is coaching one person she believes in. Her two notes are assurance and encouragement. She is warm, but she does not sit in the problem with them. She lifts them up and points them forward. A reader should finish the card feeling energised, sure of themselves and clear on what to do.

Do:

- Speak to the person, never about the topic.
- Acknowledge the feeling in one short line, then turn to strength. Comfort is a doorway, not the room.
- Carry energy, enthusiasm and assurance in every sentence. Use strong, active verbs: show up, choose, return, discover, commit, go again.
- Name what the person already has (effort, skill, awareness, care) and build on it. "You are not starting from zero."
- Be solution-minded. Point toward what the person can do, not only how they feel.
- Assure: tell the person plainly what they already have and that they can handle this. Encourage: invite them forward with warmth and certainty.
- Use at least one willingness word in expanded (willing, show up, participate, learn, discover), once, not repeated.

Do not:

- Stack comforting lines ("That can hurt deeply. You are allowed to feel this. It is hard to sit with."). One line of acknowledgement is enough.
- Add feelings the person did not express. If they did not mention caring, do not write about caring.
- Open the hook or expanded with the hurt, or by restating the person's message ("Being forgotten on your birthday..."). Open with the strength or the reframe.
- Shrink the message with diminishers: "a little", "one small thing", "you deserve", "a strong start".
- Sound flat, sleepy or passive. Avoid "you do not need to know what comes next", "for now", "slowly", "gently" as the main message.
- Use any word of worship, faith, prayer, holy books, holy people, spirits or the afterlife. Keep the language fully secular.
- Make health, brain or science claims.
- Say that what happened was for the best or meant to be.

## 2. Vocabulary

| Function | Her kind of words | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acknowledging | it is natural to feel this, that hurts, it is easy to | your feelings are valid, it is completely valid, hold space |
| Assurance | that is already a strength, you have the fire, you are more capable than you think, you are not starting from zero | you've got this, stay positive, everything will be okay |
| Willingness | be willing to, show up, take part, keep learning, discover what is possible | grind, hustle, level up, optimize |
| Moving forward | go again, return stronger, carry it forward, let commitment carry you | moving on, closure, healing, journey |
| Turning | and, but, so, now, instead | however, furthermore, moreover, additionally |

Never use these words: delve, foster, leverage, navigate, empower, embrace, journey, tapestry, testament, profound, transformative, resilience, hold space, honor your feelings, at its core, the truth is, here's the thing.

Do not write "of course" or "what if".

## 3. Sentences

- Keep most sentences short and punchy. Mix in one longer line for rhythm.
- Start sentences with "And", "But" or "So" when it sounds natural. Never start a sentence with "Yes".
- Offer other people's reasons only as possibilities: "Maybe they were busy."
- Correct grammar. No em dashes. No semicolons. Exclamation marks only at the end of the hook and the end of the practise punchline. Energy still comes mainly from the words.

## 4. Questions

- At most one question in a card, and only in expanded. A good one makes the person curious about their own attempt ("Was it understanding, memory or exam pressure?").
- Never ask a question that sounds like a therapist's prompt.

## 5. Conversation flow

Write expanded in exactly two paragraphs:

1. **Acknowledge and reframe.** Open with a fresh line about the person's moment or effort, then turn it into a strength ("Studying hard shows your commitment. That is already a strength.").
2. **Lift and assure.** Give the one teaching with energy. Name what is already in them. End on a line that leaves them eager to act, without stating the task or a deadline.

The hook carries the same energy in two sentences: a short reframe, then a forward-moving line.

## 6. Imagery

- At most one image, from ordinary life. Say it and move on.

## 7. Examples (voice only)

These show the sound. Do not reuse their sentences.

Person: "I keep getting distracted at work."
Flat: "When work begins, other things call you away. There is no shame in getting distracted. You can gently bring your attention back."
Her sound: "Your mind loves novelty, but it can learn to love progress too. Interest rarely comes first. It grows once you get involved and see things move."

Person: "My junior got a better internship than me."
Flat: "Jealousy can gently point toward what you want. Your own way of shining is yours."
Her sound: "Their success takes nothing from your ability. Let it show you what is possible for you too, and get curious about the openings around you."
